Where to send: Başkentgaz's corporate contact channel (the contact form on the website, or the unit the call centre points to). Başkentgaz is a private company and not covered by the Right to Information Act, so this is a request letter, not a formal application.

**Subject:** Request to publish and share natural gas outage information

Dear Başkentgaz,

I am [Name Surname], and as part of a DevOps internship at [school / company] I am building an open source, non-commercial project called "Kesinti Haritası" (outage map, https://github.com/agern28/kesinti-haritasi). It shows electricity, water and natural gas outages published by official sources on a single map. Each record shows the name of the source institution and a link to the original announcement.

We would like to show planned and unplanned natural gas outages in Ankara on the map as well. We couldn't find this information on your website. We know you notify your subscribers by SMS and e-mail.

We are asking for one of the following:

1. Publishing planned outage announcements (district, neighbourhood, start and estimated end time) as a page on your website,
2. or sharing the same information through an API or a regularly updated data file.

How we will use the data:

- Planned outage information at most every 15 minutes, fault information at most every 5 minutes, with a User-Agent that contains the project name and a contact address.
- The data is shown unchanged, with Başkentgaz and a link to the original announcement as the source.
- No personal or subscriber data is processed.
- You can stop the access at any time. If you ask, we stop using the data immediately.
